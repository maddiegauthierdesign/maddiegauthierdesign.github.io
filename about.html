About Me

Let me introduce myself...

Hi! Nice to meet you! My name is Maddie Gauthier!
I'm a current senior at the University of St. Thomas in St.Paul, MN. I plan to graduate in May 2026. I'm pursuing a BA degree in Digital Media Design and a minor in Business Administration. It was around my junior year of high school when I discovered design was calling my name. I have always loved art, so I wanted to find a career that allowed me to be creative and that I knew could make a difference. I discovered graphic design and haven't looked back since. I currently am working for St. Thomas Libraries as a Marketing and Communications Student Intern, and have been learning a lot about how to apply my digital design skills into the real world.

Artist Statement
I aim to make creative and innovative solutions for the projects I work on, through my work as a designer. As I continue on my path as a designer, I will continue to make work that meets professional standards yet remains fun and colorful. I grew up with a love for creativity that came through in mediums of pencil, colored pencil, water color, and charcoal. My favorite projects in art were always when we were allowed to experiment with the colors and textures. I love to use color in my designs because it gives the room for extra creativity. It allows me to properly express my style of design and integrate fun aesthetics. I am so grateful that I have found a career that I can freely express m imagination within a professional setting. This need to be creative reflected into my decision to pursue a career in graphic design, where I had the opportunity to make art in a professional and business setting. I currently work in Adobe suites, Canva, and Procreate as my design mediums, and that is where most of my strengths lie. I am hoping to continue my design career as a graphic designer in the professional world, where I can showcase my skills and integrate my creativity into brands and clients I work with.
