Cosmic Artware Branding

Cosmic Artware is a window cling and art company that I was asked to create a new logo for. I also took some time to create a business card, a black and white logo, and a new insert for the actual window clings. It was interesting to create a logo from scratch and I learned a lot about what I can accomplish if given the time and confidence to do so.

Music Festival Poster

This was a mock poster I made for a hypothetical music festival in the Twin Cities. I had this idea because I saw a few visually pleasing music festival posters on Pinterest, but also because I think St.Paul/Minneapolis should have more live music festivals.

Study Room Flyer

This flyer was made for work at my student intern job at the Libraries for the Keffer Library in Minneapolis. This was made for the study rooms on campus reminding students to clean up after themselves. The trash cans were located outside the study room and this often led students to leave it a mess. I made this flyer to direct them to where the trash cans and recycle bins were so this problem could be solved.

Sparkle Soda

This is a project I was assigned for my Graphic Design class in the Fall of 2024. We were given a choice of a product template from Photoshop and I chose to go with a can design. I really like this retro-minimalist style of design so that's what I incorporated into my company's aesthetics.

The Cozy Tea Room Logo

This was another mock company I made to practice my design skills, called "The Cozy Tea Room". The idea behind it is that it would be similar to a coffee shop's vibe, where you can order a drink and then sit down and study/work, but instead of coffee they would mainly serve tea, (and yes, maybe some coffee). I wanted the company's aesthetic to feel cozy and cute and have lots of plants incorporated into the designs.

Spider-Man Movie Poster

I made this movie poster for Spider-Man Across the Spider-Verse in 2024, for a Digital Media class assignment. I used Adobe Photoshop and images from the web to create this. Since the movie has this villain called "The Spot", I used his time-bending spots to showcase a few of the other characters. Just using his spots instead of the actual character causes "The Spot" to haunt the narrative, which was my intention

The Sticky Strawberries Poster

I made this poster in the summer of 2023 for one of my Coursera Graphic Design Certificate classes. In the end I learned a lot about both Adobe and my style of design.
